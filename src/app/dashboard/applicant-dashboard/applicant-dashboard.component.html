General instructions for users as self(worker/employee)/ Group of Workers/Legal Heir/Nominee /Legal Practitioner/Authorized Representative

  • This portal is primarily designed for filing industrial disputes/claims/grievances ,etc., against establishments/employers that fall under Central jurisdiction and users are advised not to file issues related to EPF/ESIC
  • After successful registration, users will receive a link on their registered email and mobile number to set their username and password.
  • Users can file applications:
    • As self(worker/employee)
    • As a Group of Workers
    • As a Legal Heir/Legal Practitioner/Authorized Representative/Nominee
